Privacy

Last updated July 2026

Morrow AI is the company behind a small family of focused tools, including CreatorHQ. This page covers the Morrow AI website itself; each product may have its own, more specific privacy page covering how that product handles your data.

This website

This site (morrowai.app) uses Vercel's built-in analytics to see aggregate traffic — page views and which links get clicked. It's cookieless and doesn't identify you individually.

Our products

Each Morrow AI product is built to work with as little data collection as possible, and most store your data locally on your own device rather than on a server. See the specific product's own privacy page for details — for example, CreatorHQ's privacy page.
